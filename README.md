# 🏴 7-0 Volta a Catalunya Draft

Draft 5 legendary Volta a Catalunya cyclists across 6 eras (1911-present, 115 years) and try to win every stage of the Catalan week race. 84 legends · 6 eras · 7 stages · 1 goal: 7-0.

**X-0 Sports Draft #18** — Catalan stripes (Senyera) themed, week-stage template (like CdD/TdS/Tirreno/Paris-Nice).

## How to Play

1. **Pick 5 legends** from any era (1911-1959 to 2020-now)
2. **Race 7 stages** of authentic 2026 Volta a Catalunya calendar (Barcelona → Mataró → Montserrat → La Molina → Andorra → Vallter 2000 → Barcelona Montjuïc)
3. **Win all 7 stages** = Senyera (Catalan flag jersey) glory

## The Legends

84 legends across 6 eras of the Volta — from **Mariano Cañardo** (1911-1959 founder era) to **Tadej Pogačar** (2020-now).

Era distribution (14 per era):
- Era 1 (1911-1959): Cañardo, Leducq, Coppi, Bartali, Bobet, Anquetil, Bahamontes...
- Era 2 (1960-1979): Merckx, Gimondi, Ocaña, De Vlaeminck, Van Looy, Zoetemelk...
- Era 3 (1980-1999): Indurain, Lejarreta, Delgado, Cubino, Escartín, Heras...
- Era 4 (2000-2009): Armstrong, Ullrich, Valverde, Sastre, Samuel Sánchez...
- Era 5 (2010-2019): Contador, Froome, Rodríguez, Quintana, A. Yates, S. Yates, Pinot, Majka...
- Era 6 (2020-now): Pogačar, Vingegaard, Evenepoel, Roglič, Ayuso, Mas, Carapaz...

## Stage Format

7 stages:
- 1 ITT (Barcelona Prologue)
- 1 flat (Mataró Sprint)
- 2 hills (Montserrat, Barcelona Montjuïc)
- 3 mountain (La Molina, Andorra, Vallter 2000)

3 mountain stages = Volta's defining feature (Pyrenean summit finishes)

## Era Synergy

Pick multiple drivers from the same era → **+1.5 OVR bonus** (max 6 drivers from one era).
